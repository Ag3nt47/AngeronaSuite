## Final reliability changes

The optional monitor's worker waits for a single-use admission event. No native
work is admitted until Thread.start returns successfully. An uncertain start
creates a sticky hold for that optional monitor while the Core's other workers
continue. That failure never grants a retrying native owner or extra authority.

The scanner's GUI quit is queued only after authenticated STOP and successful
cleanup. Cleanup failure remains explicit rather than reporting successful
shutdown. Maintenance process ownership recognizes at most 16 harmless Python
prefix switches before its known entry point. A -c command or unknown entry is
not confused with an owned Angerona process.

The guarded publisher's documentation check previously attempted ambient Git

The corrected hosted build retains its own acceptance result.

## AI provider self-test and host assessment

## Native atomic rename follow-up

A controlled native A/B comparison reproduced ERROR_INVALID_NAME in all 16
counted-only rename buffers with nonzero suffix bytes outside the supplied
extent. All 16 explicitly terminated variants succeeded. The production encoder
now includes its UTF-16 terminator inside the supplied buffer while keeping
FileNameLength exclusive of that terminator. Path, ACL, handle and replacement
admission remain unchanged. Native errno identity is preserved under literal
operation labels; existing underlying error details remain private.

The final native suite passed 93 top-level tests with one explicit export-only
skip, and Go vet exited 0. Both scopes verified current source pins and native
retirement. A longer two-case stress scope reached its 240-second diagnostic
budget after 59 completed passing iterations; it is retained as incomplete,
not a completed 50-round acceptance. Its sampled pending CreateProcess call
does not identify a deadlock or prove the cause of the longer duration.

A separate bounded repeat completed three rounds of each originally failing
case, six passes, with unchanged source pins and verified native retirement.
This smaller completed scope does not replace the retained extended timeout.

The unsigned PC bundle was rebuilt from all 17 current recorded source inputs.
Its image and metadata hashes match. A valid --validate-only call remained
inactive with exit 5 before installed authority access. No signing, enrollment,
service activation or saved setting change followed from this maintenance.

## Fresh PC source observation

The canonical hidden-console Safe Startup launch completed its initial painted
and responding dashboard handshake on the PC checkout. The actual Core was
identified independently from the virtual-environment launcher by its native
creation time and image. Four read-only samples over one minute retained that
same Core generation, advancing Core and watchdog progress, scanner status and
two fresh aggregate reports identifying version 1.15.1. Diagnostic query owners
retired with their handles closed; saved Live Debug remained disabled.

This was a Chill-mode Source Observe session, not installed Protect acceptance.
Source-update ownership remained unavailable, and the optional native monitor
reported source-disabled as expected. Existing HIGH review evidence was
preserved. There were no active critical alerts in the sampled aggregate;
that is not a clean-device or complete-coverage verdict. Startup readiness,
peer liveness, telemetry continuity and protection authority remain distinct.

A real validly signed and enrolled positive deployment remains unaccepted.
The optional observer shares the Core job and has no independent recovery.
Same-user source tampering, administrator/kernel compromise, whole-root replay
and Source Observe network enforcement remain documented limits.
