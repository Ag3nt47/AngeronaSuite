## Final reliability changes

The optional monitor's worker waits for a single-use admission event. No native
work is admitted until Thread.start returns successfully. An uncertain start
creates a sticky hold for that optional monitor while the Core's other workers
continue. That failure never grants a retrying native owner or extra authority.

The scanner's GUI quit is queued only after authenticated STOP and successful
cleanup. Cleanup failure remains explicit rather than reporting successful
shutdown. Maintenance process ownership recognizes at most 16 harmless Python
prefix switches before its known entry point. A -c command or unknown entry is
not confused with an owned Angerona process.
