## Trust boundary that remains

An editable source run, its same-user keys and its user-mode peers cannot establish immunity to a same-user debugger, administrator or compromised kernel. Native rule presence does not prove effective firewall policy. Forward integrity, rollback freshness and protected service authority need independently held keys/counters or a separately administered deployment; local HMACs alone cannot provide those guarantees.
