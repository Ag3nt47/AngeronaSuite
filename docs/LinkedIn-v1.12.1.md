## Post

Angerona v1.12.1 is ready to ship — the largest defensive hardening update to
the project so far. Its exact release gate is 2678 passed,
13 intentional platform skips, and 0 failures.

The headline addition is **SentinelLens**, a local-first autonomous threat-
hunting and log-anomaly workspace. It ingests bounded Syslog, Windows Event,
NetFlow, JSON, and live Angerona EventBus evidence; maps process, network, file,
technique, and correlation relationships; and explains exactly why each anomaly
was selected. Every node and finding is clickable, and remediation stays
proposal-only until exact evidence is reviewed.

This release also adds a guided **Auto Adapt** workflow, an authenticated Windows
Firewall recovery baseline, expanded one-button defensive simulations, sortable
and clickable assurance views, and governed source evidence whenever a capability
is below 100%. A verified line is highlighted when provenance proves it; missing
fields use an explicit class-declaration fallback, and unavailable source is never
guessed. Five new adversarial engineering loops attacked crash boundaries,
object-identity races, telemetry loss, forged state, rollback, and misleading
health claims; reopened findings were patched and re-tested instead of being
papered over.

Angerona remains deliberately defensive and local-first: no hack-back, no live
exploitation, no credential collection, and no claim that a user-mode research
suite is invulnerable. The goal is transparent engineering—show the evidence,
show the gap, and fail closed when authority is incomplete.
