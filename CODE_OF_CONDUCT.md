# Community code of conduct

Angerona welcomes constructive participation from defenders, learners,
researchers, operators, and developers of all backgrounds and experience
levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while respecting documented safety and privacy boundaries.
- Share only data, systems, and test targets you are authorized to use.
- Help keep discussions accessible to newcomers and technically useful.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, credential or private-telemetry
exposure, unsafe offensive payloads, instructions for unauthorized access, and
deliberate disruption are not accepted in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject changes, or
temporarily/permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the
community or the project. Report sensitive conduct concerns privately through
the repository's security/contact channel rather than escalating them publicly.
Maintainers should handle reports discreetly, consistently, and without
retaliation.
